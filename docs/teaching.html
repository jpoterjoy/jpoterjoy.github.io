Teaching

Courses at the University of Maryland and lectures at data assimilation schools.

Courses

Instructor

AOSC 470/600: Synoptic Meteorology

University of Maryland (cross-listed undergraduate/graduate course) · Fall 2019 – present

Topics: numerical modeling and observational analysis, data assimilation, governing equations of the atmosphere, quasi-geostrophic theory, frontogenesis, extratropical waves and cyclones, and weather map discussions

Instructor

AOSC 472/602: Mesoscale Meteorology

University of Maryland (cross-listed undergraduate/graduate course) · Spring 2020 – present

Topics: mesoscale instabilities, semi-geostrophic approximations, the planetary boundary layer, mesoscale air-mass boundaries, convective initiation, convective organization, mesoscale convective systems, and tornadoes

Guest Lecturer

METR 6313: Advanced Topics in Data Assimilation

University of Oklahoma · Spring 2017

Lecture topics: introduction to particle filters and the local particle filter

Co-Instructor

METEO 597B: Data Assimilation

Pennsylvania State University · Spring 2013

Lecture topics: optimal interpolation, EnKF, 3DVar, 4DVar, adjoint sensitivity analysis, ensemble sensitivity analysis, observation impact, parameter estimation, and hybrid data assimilation

Additional tasks: helped develop the course curriculum, and designed and graded programming lab assignments

Guest Lecturer

METEO 526: Numerical Weather Prediction

Pennsylvania State University · Spring 2011

Lecture topics: ensemble Kalman filters and a Lorenz (1963) model tutorial

Summer schools and training workshops
